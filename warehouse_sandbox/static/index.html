Coordinates are metres. Times are seconds after facility.start_time. Each pallet has its own ID; SKU IDs can repeat.

Planning and observations: scheduled_arrival_seconds is the arrival estimate available to the policy. arrival_seconds is the actual arrival. observed_departure_seconds is used for calibration. appointment_id groups orders loaded on one truck visit. Optional priority is an integer from 1 to 10 (default 1) and affects the original optimizer's urgency weight.

Temperature is optional on locations and inventory, and defaults to ambient. Ownership, temperatures, capacity, references and requested quantities are validated during import.

Model interpretation

Recommendation policy

Original optimizer · Phase 1. The copied PrePositionScheduler generates candidates using MovementScorer and FeasibilityEngine. The replay harness models their operating consequences.

Current policy model

Loading begins after truck arrival. Original optimizer recommendations use released orders and available staging capacity.

Not calibrated

Supply observed departure times to compare baseline estimates with historical results.

Simulation assumptions
  • Results estimate a counterfactual. They are not proof of actual savings.
  • No WMS writeback or equipment dispatch is available.
